Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Installers and consultants in our network pay us a referral fee when we introduce a qualified customer, which is how we keep the service — and our calculators — free for you. It never changes the price you're quoted.

How are installers vetted?

Partners must hold the licences, insurance and manufacturer certifications required in their region, provide references and maintain good customer reviews. We remove partners who receive repeated complaints.

Will I be bombarded with calls?

No. Your request goes to a maximum of three matched companies, and you can tell us you prefer email only.

I rent — can I still save?

Often, yes: community solar, plug-in balcony panels where permitted, portable batteries, smart thermostats and energy audits. Tell us in the notes and we'll match accordingly.

Are you an installer or utility?

No. Carbon.Energy is an independent comparison and information service. See our disclaimer.
