    How this calculator works

    Your footprint is the sum of seven categories. Home energy multiplies your electricity use by your country's average grid intensity (from Our World in Data / Ember) and your heating fuels by standard combustion factors — 0.183 kg CO₂e per kWh of natural gas, 2.54 kg per litre of heating oil and 1.56 kg per litre of LPG — then divides by the people in your home. Car emissions use 2.31 kg per litre of petrol and 2.68 kg per litre of diesel; electric cars use your grid. Flights use typical round-trip values per passenger including non-CO₂ effects. Food and goods & services use diet and spending archetypes from life-cycle studies.

    The 2.3 tonne reference reflects the per-person level broadly consistent with limiting warming to 1.5 °C by 2030. Most people in high-income countries are at 8–20 tonnes, so the biggest wins usually come from heating, driving and flying — which is why the plan ranks actions by tonnes saved, not by how virtuous they feel.
