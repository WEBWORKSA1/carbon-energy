Carbon.Energy exists to answer two questions quickly and honestly: how much carbon does my energy use cause? and what's the smartest way to cut it — and my bills — from here?

Most people are told to "reduce their footprint" without ever seeing a number, let alone a number for their own grid, their own roof or their own car. Meanwhile, the open data needed to answer those questions has never been better. We connect the two.

What we do

  • Live and refreshed data. Our pipeline pulls Our World in Data's energy and CO₂ datasets, NOAA's Mauna Loa CO₂ record and clean-energy news feeds on a schedule, regenerates 200+ country profiles, and updates the site automatically. Live grid data streams straight from the operator's API.
  • Free calculators. Personal and business footprints, solar production with real irradiance for your location, EV and heat-pump comparisons, flight emissions and energy conversions.
  • Plain-English guides. Written by our editorial team, sourced, and updated when the evidence changes.
  • Free quotes. When you're ready to act, we connect you with up to three vetted installers or consultants.

How we make money

We're funded by display advertising (Google AdSense), referral fees from installer and consultant partners, clearly labelled sponsorships, and reader support. None of these influence our data, rankings or calculator outputs. Read our disclaimer for details.

Editorial standards

  • Every figure has a source; emission factors are documented on the page that uses them.
  • We correct errors promptly and note material updates.
  • We separate facts from forecasts and present uncertainty honestly.
  • We are non-partisan: we report what policies do, not which party to support.
