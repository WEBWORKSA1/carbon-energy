Why installers join the lead partner programme

Our requests come from people who have already run their own numbers: they know their bill, their roof shading, their timeline and roughly what a system should save. Every lead includes a lead score (ownership, timeline, bill size and contact completeness) and the inputs the customer used in our calculators, so your first call starts at the quote, not at "what is solar?".

  • Coverage by postcode radius, service and property type
  • Exclusive leads available in many regions
  • Monthly caps so you never pay for more than you can install
  • Consent captured on every request

Sponsorship standards

Sponsored content is always labelled. Sponsors never influence data, rankings or calculator outputs. We decline advertisers whose claims we can't verify, including offset products without independent certification.
