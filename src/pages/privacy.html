This policy explains how Carbon.Energy ("we", "us") handles information when you use this website. We aim to collect as little as possible.

1. Information you give us

When you submit a form (quote request, contact, newsletter, donation pledge, contest entry or job application) we receive the details you enter, the page you submitted from and the time. Forms are delivered to our inbox through a form-processing service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond to you and provide the service you asked for.

Quote requests: if you ask for quotes and give consent, we share your request with up to three vetted installers or consultants who serve your area so they can contact you about it. Those partners are independent companies with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your information to unrelated third parties.

2. Calculators

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser. Some preferences (theme, country, last calculator inputs used to pre-fill a quote form) are stored in your browser's local storage on your device and are not sent to us unless you submit a form.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

When you first visit, you can choose "Essential only", in which case we request non-personalised ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may also see a Google-certified consent message.

4. Third-party services

  • Data APIs called from your browser: Open-Meteo (geocoding and solar data), Carbon Intensity API (live grid data), GDELT Project (news). These services receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.
  • YouTube videos load in privacy-enhanced mode and only after you press play.
  • Google Fonts for typography.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical request data.

5. Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them for and to meet legal obligations, typically no longer than 24 months.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, to withdraw consent and to object to processing. To exercise them, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request". You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Contest entrants aged 13–17 require guardian consent.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
