Reading the rankings

Grid intensity tells you how dirty each kilowatt-hour is — it's what matters for an EV, a heat pump or a data centre. Low-carbon share adds nuclear to renewables. CO₂ per person captures the whole economy, including transport, industry and heating, so a country can have a very clean grid and still high per-capita emissions (or the reverse). Use both lenses before drawing conclusions.
