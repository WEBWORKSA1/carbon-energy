Orientation matters

South-facing roofs (north-facing in the southern hemisphere) are ideal. East/west splits lose 10–20% but match morning and evening use better.

Self-consumption is king

Every kWh you use yourself saves the full retail price. Exported power often earns far less — that's when a battery or EV charging schedule pays off.
