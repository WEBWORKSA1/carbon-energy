Scope 1, 2 and 3 in plain English

Scope 1 covers emissions from fuel you burn yourself — boilers, generators and company vehicles. Scope 2 is the electricity, heat and steam you buy. Scope 3 is everything else in your value chain: purchased goods, business travel, commuting, waste, logistics and the use of the products you sell. For most service businesses Scope 3 is 70–90% of the total, which is why customers increasingly ask suppliers for it.
